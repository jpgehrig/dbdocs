## 4.123 DBA_ARGUMENTS

`DBA_ARGUMENTS` lists the arguments of the functions and procedures that are available in the database. Its columns are the same as those in `ALL_ARGUMENTS`.

- "[ALL_ARGUMENTS](ALL_ARGUMENTS.md)"

- "[DBA_PROCEDURES](DBA_PROCEDURES.md)" for information about the functions and procedures that are available in the database
