# Description of the illustration gsmug_vm_009

This graphic is depicts an APAC Region and an EMEA Region. The APAC Region is a vertical rectangle on the left. Inside are cylinders representing databases and rectangular boxes representing servers. The EMEA Region is a vertical rectangle on the right. A GDS Pool named SALES POOL is located horizontally across the first row of databases and servers in both the APAC and EMEA regions. The HR POOL GDS pool encompasses the bottom row of servers and databases.
